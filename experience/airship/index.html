PLAYER EXPERIENCE AND COLLABORATION

Make an available action discoverable.

A building can be clickable and still be missed.

What I noticed

In a simulation-management discussion, the proposed interaction was to click an unbuilt building to unlock it or read its requirements. I noticed that a new player might not know when to click, or that the building was interactive.

What I did

I proposed supporting tasks and guidance, together with a clear visual cue on buildings that could be unlocked. The designer agreed with the suggestion.

What I learned

I use implementation discussions to ask what an interaction should achieve for the player. This example records a design proposal and the agreement; it does not claim a measured usability improvement.
